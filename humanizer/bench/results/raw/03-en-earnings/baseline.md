# Northwind Robotics Reports Third-Quarter Results

Northwind Robotics reported third-quarter revenue of $412 million for the quarter ended September 30, up 23% year-over-year and above analyst expectations of $389 million. Net income rose to $47.2 million, or $0.94 per diluted share, from $31.5 million, or $0.63 per share, in the same quarter last year.

"We are seeing unprecedented demand for our autonomous fulfillment systems," said CEO Marta Lindqvist during the earnings call. "Our order backlog now stands at $1.8 billion, which gives us exceptional visibility into 2027."

Revenue from the company's flagship PickStream platform grew 61%, while the services segment expanded 18%. International sales rose to 34% of total revenue, from 28% a year ago.

Gross margin contracted to 41.3% from 43.1%, which CFO David Chen attributed to elevated component costs and tariff-related pressures. The company also announced a workforce reduction of 3%, approximately 210 positions, as part of a broader restructuring initiative expected to save $25 million annually.

Northwind raised its full-year revenue guidance to a range of $1.58 billion to $1.62 billion, from its prior forecast of $1.52 billion to $1.57 billion. Shares rose 6.8% in after-hours trading following the announcement.
