# Northwind Robotics reports third-quarter results

Northwind Robotics reported third-quarter revenue of $412 million for the quarter ended September 30, up 23% year-over-year and above analyst expectations of $389 million. Net income rose to $47.2 million, or $0.94 per diluted share, from $31.5 million, or $0.63 per share, a year earlier.

"We are seeing unprecedented demand for our autonomous fulfillment systems," said CEO Marta Lindqvist during the earnings call. "Our order backlog now stands at $1.8 billion, which gives us exceptional visibility into 2027."

Revenue from the company's flagship PickStream platform grew 61%, while the services segment expanded 18%. International sales accounted for 34% of total revenue, up from 28% a year ago.

Gross margin fell to 41.3% from 43.1%. CFO David Chen attributed the decline to elevated component costs and tariff-related pressures. The company also announced a workforce reduction of 3%, or approximately 210 positions, as part of a broader restructuring initiative expected to save $25 million annually.

Northwind raised its full-year revenue guidance to $1.58 billion to $1.62 billion, from its prior forecast of $1.52 billion to $1.57 billion. Shares rose 6.8% in after-hours trading following the announcement, highlighting investor confidence in the company's trajectory.
